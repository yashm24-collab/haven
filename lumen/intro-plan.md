# Haven startup animation: "Welcome home"

**What it is:** the first thing the TV shows after power-on. A 5 s welcome, then Home.

**Angle:** the TV doesn't boot, it comes home. The scene wakes, the cabin light comes on, and the screen says "Welcome home".

**Hook (0–2 s):** the landscape rising out of the dark, with the cabin window lighting up.

**Tone:** polished (brag-slim preset): restrained, long holds, soft fades, one easing, no bounce.

**Identity:** the owner's three landscapes, chosen by time of day. Instrument Serif in warm white `#f3ece1`, with an amber `#e6b06a` hairline (the iso-glow accent: only the live light glows).

**Sound:** none. A chime at power-on is not calm late at night (Haven PRD §1). Can be added as an option later.

| Frames (24 fps) | Time | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 | 0–1.25 s | Fade up from black (LG's splash is black, so the start is seamless); the camera eases from 1.07× to 1.0× over the whole intro |
| 18–46 | 0.75–1.9 s | The cabin window light blooms: someone's home |
| 34–62 | 1.4–2.6 s | "Welcome" then "home" resolve from blur, rising 12 px, staggered by 10 frames |
| 52–76 | 2.2–3.2 s | The amber hairline draws out from the centre and glows |
| 62–92 | 2.6–3.8 s | Hold: two words, fully settled for 1.25 s (brag-slim: about 0.3 s per word, minimum) |
| 92–106 | 3.8–4.4 s | Text and hairline dissolve; the window bloom eases back |
| 106–120 | 4.4–5 s | The plain scene, identical to frame 0 of the Home loop; the TV fades in Home's dock and orb |

**Checks before the full render:** stills of every beat and mid-transition for all three lights. Text contrast must hold over the bright morning sky.
